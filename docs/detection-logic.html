1. System overview

The integration turns raw target coordinates from LD2450-based mmWave radars (Everything Presence Lite, Screek Human Sensor 2A, generic ESPHome) into four kinds of output:

  1. Zone presence: an occupancy binary sensor per user-drawn polygon (plus a target-count sensor and, for zones several radars see, per-radar "seen by" diagnostics).
  2. Exit passages: an event entity per exit zone firing arrived / left.
  3. Head count: people home, per floor and per area, maintained by conservation of people (tokens) across areas, floors and the home's true exits.
  4. Advisory analysis (panel only): suggested zones, ghost detection, and tidy-up questions derived from recorder history. It never changes detection by itself; the user accepts a suggestion, which edits the saved zones.

Zone presence and the head count are independent consumers of the same tracked targets. The head count never alters zone states, and zone filters (delays, holds) never affect the head count.

1.1 Event loop

Everything runs on Home Assistant's event loop with no worker threads. Inputs are state-change events: coordinate sensors, doors, locks, evidence entities and vacuums. Timers drive deadlines:

  • the frame flush, COALESCE_S after the first update;
  • the group deadline: the earliest of the zone delays, hold expiry and lost-track expiry;
  • the head count tick, every TICK_S = 5 s;
  • the contact-held check, CONTACT_MIN_S after a contact opens;
  • the radar range refresh, every 60 s.

The engines (engine/*.py) are pure Python with time passed in. The same code runs live, in history replay (tools/) and in tests. frontend/engine.js and frontend/tracker.js are line-by-line ports used by the panel's preview, and test vectors keep the two languages in lock-step.

2.3 Discovery and geometry

Each radar is a device recognised by profile: Everything Presence, Screek 2A or generic ESPHome. Its targets map slots to X/Y sensor entities.

  • Range: max_distance comes from the device's range number every 60 s, defaulting to 8000 mm. A range with no unit is read by its size, since one firmware reported centimetres.
  • Field of view: ±FOV_HALF_ANGLE_DEG = 60°.
  • Units: coordinate units come from the sensors' unit_of_measurement. A unitless sensor falls back to a sibling's unit, then to the unit remembered in the entry.
  • Firmware exclusions: exclusion zones set on the device itself are only offered for import in the panel. The radar still reports targets inside them, and they have no effect until imported as an exclude zone.

3. Stage 1 — frame assembly

Source: engine/frames.py:FrameAssembler, home.py:HomeRuntime._on_state/_flush

FR-1Firmwares publish each target's X and Y as separate state updates, a few milliseconds apart, and HA drops updates whose value did not change. The first update after a quiet period opens a window of COALESCE_S = 50 ms. Every update inside the window belongs to one frame, built from the latest value of every coordinate entity when the window closes.
FR-2A slot is a target only if both X and Y parse as numbers and Y ≠ 0. An empty slot reads (0, 0); X = 0 is a valid boresight reading.
FR-3A frame is available if any coordinate entity parses. Values are converted to mm with the entity's unit (mm, cm, m, in, ft; none = mm). The frame time is the newest last_updated in the window.
FR-4At start-up, current states seed the assembler and one frame is built and evaluated at once. Targets already in view at start-up are not passages.

4. Stage 2 — per-radar feed

Source: home.py:RadarFeed.process

Per frame, per radar
  • Frame available now and the previous one was not? 
    frame.available and not self._available
    • yesStart grace: grace_until = t + reconnect_grace_s (home option, default 0)
  • Inside reconnect grace? 
    t < grace_until
    • yeskept = () (suppressed: the junk a radar reports right after reconnecting is ignored)
    • nokept = targets not inside any of this radar's exclusion polygons (radar frame, even-odd rule)
Exclusions apply per radar, before fusion, in that radar's own frame. An area excluded on radar A is still seen by an aligned radar B. That is intended: B can confirm a real person where A sees a reflection.

5. Stage 3 — alignment groups and transforms

Source: engine/align.py, home.py:GroupRuntime

AL-1A radar may store aligned_to = {radar_id, angle_deg, reflect, offset_mm}: the rigid transform from its frame into the reference radar's frame. This is an optional X mirror, then a rotation, then a translation.
AL-2Radars connected by alignments form a group. The anchor is the root of the chain: the member not aligned to another member, or for a cycle the first in configuration order. Each member's transform into the anchor frame comes from a breadth-first walk, using inverse transforms against the arrow direction.
AL-3Each group runs one tracker and one zone engine in the anchor frame. All its members' include zones, exits and spots are transformed into that frame. A radar with no alignment is a group of one with the identity transform: exactly the single-radar behaviour.
AL-4Coverage of a zone: the zone's own radar, plus every group member whose field of view contains a sample of the zone polygon. Samples are the vertices, edge midpoints and centroid; "in view" means y > 0, within range and within ±60°. With ≥2 covering radars, "seen by" sensors are created.

6. Stage 4 — people tracker

Source: engine/tracker.py:Tracker.fuse (port: frontend/tracker.js)

Radar slots are not identities. When the LD2450 drops a target (someone sitting still), the remaining ones are renumbered: slot 2 becomes slot 1, a 5 m "jump" in one frame. A person who reappears gets any free slot. The tracker turns observations (already in the group frame) into tracks with stable integer ids, which the zone engine uses as slots.

Tracker: one fuse() call (all observations of the group at time t)
  • 1. Expire lost tracks past their TTL → ids reported in dropped.
  • 2. For each observation: its (radar, slot) belonged to a track, and it lies within reach of that track? 
    reach = NOISE_MM + min(VMAX_MM_S · dt, MAX_STEP_MM)   # 500 + min(2500·dt, 1500)
    • yesassigned to that track
    • nounassigned (slot renumbered or reused)
  • 3. A track with ≥2 observations (several radars): any lies more than SPLIT_MM = 1200 from the observation of its oldest member?
    • yessplit off → unassigned
  • 4. Pair leftovers, nearest first (greedy over all candidate pairs sorted by distance):
    • with a previous track that already has a member from the observation's radar, within reach(MAX_STEP_MM) (a renumbered slot);
    • with a previous track of other radars only, within MERGE_MM = 600 (the same person seen by a second radar);
    • with a lost track within NOISE_MM + min(VMAX_MM_S · dt, REVIVE_MM) (re-found near where they vanished; keeps the id).
    • A track never takes two observations from the same radar. A lost track is revived at most once.
  • 5. Build the tracks: position = mean of the track's observations. Members keep their order (oldest first).
  • 6. Previous tracks not continued → lost. Settled? (see the tree below)
    • Remembered as _Lost(settled). Above MAX_LOST = 32, the oldest are forgotten (reported in dropped).
  • 7. Each remaining observation: a live track of another radar within MERGE_MM?
    • yesjoins the nearest such track
    • nonew track; id = 10·radar_index + slot if free and < 1000, else the first free id ≥ 1000 → reported in born
  • 8. Merge loop: two tracks with disjoint radars within MERGE_MM?
    • yesmerge into the older one (by creation order). The absorbed id is removed from born if newborn, added to dropped if just revived (its hold moves to the survivor), else reported in gone.
Is a lost track settled? (Tracker._settled)
  • Last position inside a transient or exit zone (any group radar's)?
    • yesnot settled: forgotten after TRANSIENT_TTL_S = 20 s
    • noInside a linger zone?
      • yessettled: remembered SETTLED_TTL_S = 12 h
      • noIs it at least EDGE_MM = 600 from the side of the field of view of any radar that saw it? (Or are no radar views known?) 
        side_margin(x, y) = r · sin(60° − |atan2(x, y)|)   # distance to the nearer ±60° side
        • yessettled (vanished mid-room: still there, just not detected)
        • nonot settled (walked out of every view's side)
The far end of the range does not count as "leaving": it is usually the far wall, with a bed or desk against it.

Outputs per frame:

  • targets: track id and position, in the anchor frame;
  • gone: tracks merged away;
  • radars: track → the radar indexes that see it;
  • born: new tracks;
  • dropped: lost tracks forgotten, whose holds are released.

Between frames, the group deadline expires lost tracks and releases their holds (ZoneEngine.release).

7. Stage 5 — zone engine

Source: engine/zones.py:ZoneEngine.process (port: frontend/engine.js)

The group's engine holds every member's include zones and every exit (anchor frame). Its own grace is 0 and it has no exclusions: both were applied per feed. Each frame carries the fused targets, available = any member available, and the tracker's gone and dropped.

Zone engine: process(frame) — per include zone
  • 0. advance(t): expire holds past hold_max_s; apply delayed transitions that are due.
  • 1. exit_slots = kept targets inside any exit polygon.
  • 2. Which targets are inside? Edge margin m > 0?
    • yesHysteresis: while the zone is raw-off, a target must be ≥ m inside the edge to count. While raw-on, it counts until it is > m outside. 
      threshold = -m if rt.raw else m;  inside = signed_distance(x, y, polygon) >= threshold
    • nopoint-in-polygon (even-odd rule)
  • 3. hold_until_exit on?
    • yesUpdate holds (tree below)
    • noclear all holds
  • 4. Release holds of tracks in frame.dropped.
  • 5. count = |inside| + |holds|; raw = count > 0. When raw changes, since = t.
  • 6. raw ≠ published state, and t − since ≥ delay? (delay = on_delay_s if raw is on, else off_delay_s)
    • yesstate = raw
    • nowait; a timer fires at since + delay (next_deadline), with no frame needed
  • 7. Group presence (any target not excluded, no delays).
Hold until exit — per zone, per frame (_update_hold)
  • A slot was inside last frame and is absent now?
    • yesWas it standing in an exit last frame, or is it in gone (merged into another track)?
      • yesno hold (it left, or it was a duplicate)
      • nohold[slot] = t: it vanished inside, so it is still there, just not detected
  • A held slot is seen again (anywhere, inside or outside)?
    • yesrelease the hold. Inside, it counts as present again. Outside, it left without being seen crossing the edge.
  • The tracker forgot the slot (dropped), or t ≥ hold + hold_max_s (if > 0)?
    • yesrelease. An expiry is timestamped at hold + hold_max_s, so the off delay counts from then.
A track id is stable because of the tracker. Before the tracker existed, slot renumbering "walked" a seated person out of their zone. The hold logic depends on stable ids, and protecting that is the purpose of the vector test_desk_stays_on_while_the_radar_renumbers_targets.

8. Stage 6 — exit passages

Source: home.py:GroupRuntime._passages, HomeRuntime._passage/_on_door

PS-1left: a track present last frame, absent now and not in gone, whose last position is inside an exit. arrived: a track in born whose position is inside an exit. A track revived by the tracker is not born, so re-finding someone in a doorway is not an arrival.
Door-gated passage
  • Does the exit have a door_entity_id?
    • nofire the event
    • yesDid the door's last_changed fall within DOOR_WINDOW_S = 30 s of the passage?
      • yesfire, with attribute door_entity_id

9. Head count — model and construction

Source: headcount_runtime.py:HeadCountRuntime._build, engine/rooms.py, engine/headcount.py (module docstring)

People do not appear or vanish: they come in and leave through the home's true exits, and move between floors over the stairs. Radars lose people who sit or lie still, and rooms without a radar never see anyone. So the count is kept as tokens, one per person believed to be home, each in the region where it was last seen or inferred to be.

9.2 Rooms inside one aligned group

Source: engine/rooms.py:Rooms.room_at/split

Aligning radars merges their tracks into one picture. It does not merge the rooms. When a group spans several regions, each fused track is assigned to a region every frame:

Which room is a fused track in?
  • Inside a zone of the group (zone area, else its radar's area)? Overlapping zones: the smallest wins.
    • yesthat zone's room
    • noThe room of the nearest radar that covers the spot: side margin ≥ 0 and within its max_distance. If none covers it, the nearest radar regardless of coverage.
      • The track's previous room is within HYSTERESIS_MM = 500 of the best distance?
        • yeskeep the previous room (no flicker in a doorway)

The head count receives the split as one frame_group call. A track id that moves from one region to another between frames is handed over: the same person walking through, with bindings, pending state, path and exit marks moved along. It is not matched again.

10. Head count — engine rules

Source: engine/headcount.py:HeadCount

10.1 Frame processing

HeadCount.frame(region, tracks, t, unconfirmed)
  • 1. _tick(t): expire doubt (§10.4), confirm or expire provisional tokens (§10.7).
  • 2. Machine filter (§10.8): leave out tracks that have not moved STATIC_MM from where they appeared within STATIC_S.
  • 3. Remember unconfirmed (reflection flags from the runtime, §11.5).
  • 4. Each track that vanished: _lose (§10.5). If it was pending, discard it ("ghost").
  • 5. Each current track, in id order. Is it pending?
    • yesskip (re-checked in step 6)
    • noBound to a token that is visible on this very track?
      • yescontinuing: _bind (update position and time, moved / doubt / establish checks)
      • noAppearing (new, or re-found by the tracker). Is the token it was bound to stale? Stale means: no such token, it is visible elsewhere, it was last seen in another region, or it vanished more than REFOUND_MM = 2000 from here (track ids are reused).
        • stale_appear(prefer=None)
        • fresh_appear(prefer=token)
  • 6. Re-check every pending track of this region (§10.7).
  • 7. Mark tracks within EXIT_NEAR_MM = 400 of an outside portal (to = ()): "passed the front door at t".

10.2 A track appears: who is it?

_appear(region, track, t, pos, prefer)
  • No preferred token, and the runtime flags the track as a one-radar reflection?
    • yespending (until another radar confirms it)
  • No preferred token, not at a portal, and within SEPARATE_MM = 1500 of someone visible in this region?
    • yespending (most likely that person's track splitting)
  • At a portal (within PORTAL_MM = 1200 of it)? Which kind?
    • to outside / unobserved onlycandidate = the most recently seen unseen token in the portal's destinations (back from the garage), else in this region (lost at the door a moment ago, or seen through it)
    • to observed regions (stairs)candidate = someone from where it leads, however long they sat still; else someone from this region; else anyone who could have walked here
    • no portalcandidate = _match(region) (§10.3)
  • A preferred token (the tracker re-found its track), and (no candidate, or the candidate was seen no later than the preferred token, or the preferred token was lost ≤ REFOUND_S = 30 s ago)?
    • yescandidate = the preferred token
  • Is there a candidate?
    • yesbind it (no new person)
    • noIs this floor's exit window unsettled with direction ≠ "out"?
      • yesnew token "arrived", in doubt (counted once re-established like everyone there)
      • noComing out of an unobserved region? (a door to it was used in the last CROSS_WINDOW_S and one of its exits opened in the last UNOBSERVED_ARRIVE_S = 600 s)
        • yesnew token "arrived", counted
        • nopending, remembering the portal it appeared at

10.3 Matching an unseen token to a new track

_match(region, t, only=None, patient=False): the unseen token most likely to be a new track
  • Candidates: tokens not visible (in only, if given).
  • Skip a token in doubt from another region when this region has portals, unless only includes its region. Someone at the exit comes back through the doorway from where they are, not out of thin air.
  • Skip a token in an enclosed room (unobserved, no exits, reachable only by doorless links from observed regions; a bedroom without a radar), unless only includes it or patient. They come out through its door.
  • Can the token have reached this region? (_reach)
    • Same region: cost 0.
    • "Gate": this floor's window is unsettled, the token is not in doubt, and this region has no portals. Then a token seen more than RECENT_S = 120 s ago cannot be this track: during an exit window, a new track is someone coming in, not a sleeper.
    • Connections exist?
      • yesfewest hops along connections; a door link counts only if its door is open or changed in the last CROSS_WINDOW_S = 60 s. No path, or faster than FLOOR_TRAVEL_S = 3 s per floor → impossible. Under the gate, more than 1 hop is impossible. Cost = 1 + hops.
      • noany floor; needs FLOOR_TRAVEL_S · max(floors, 1) since last seen. Cost = 1 + floors.
  • Choose the most recently seen candidate; ties go to the lower cost (nearer). A still person who reappears is usually re-found by the tracker near where they vanished, so a new track is usually someone on the move.

10.4 Exit windows (doors to outside)

door(entity, open, t): a true exit door changes
  • Record the door's state and change time (all doors, including connection doors). Run "into unobserved" (§10.6).
  • Not an exit door of any region?
    • yesdone (connection doors only gate paths)
  • Opened?
    • yesDoes the floor have no window, or a window that closed and has settled?
      • yesnew window, opened = t, direction from the door order (§10.6)
      • nocontinue the same window. If this door's direction differs from the window's: direction = None (unknown).
      • Add it to the open doors; closed = None. Unless the direction is "in": every token on this floor, or seen on it in the last RECENT_S, goes in doubt.
    • closedWas it the last open door of the window?
      • yesclosed = t. For visible tokens in doubt, since = t (the LAG_S clock starts at the close).
On every tick: tokens in doubt whose window has closed (_expire_unknown)
  • Visible, and t − max(closed, since) ≥ LAG_S (45 s)?
    • yesestablished "still here": radars lag, and some see a little through doors
    • not − closed ≥ REESTABLISH_S (600 s), and not visible (or visible for less than LAG_S)?
      • yesremoved "left"
HC-EW-1A token in doubt is also re-established whenever it is bound to a track in a region whose floor has no unsettled window ("walked in"). This covers both seen on another floor and seen on the exit floor after it settled. Evidence and corrections also re-establish (§11). The "back" change is logged as arrived if the token was created by an arrival and has been seen for ≤ 4 · LAG_S, else back.
HC-EW-2While a floor's window is unsettled and its direction is not "in", a counted token bound to a track on that floor goes in doubt ("on the exit floor": walked down to the door while it is in use). So does a token moved there unseen ("towards the exit"). Provisional tokens are exempt.

10.5 A track vanishes

_lose(region, track, t, last position)
  • Bound to a token visible on it?
    • nonothing
    • yestoken unseen. Then:
      • Was it within EXIT_NEAR_MM of an outside portal in the last EXIT_PASS_S = 10 s? (VIRTUAL_EXITS)
        • yesIs a window on this floor open or unsettled?
          • yesextend it: closed = max(closed, t)
          • novirtual exit: a window opened and closed at t with direction "out". Tokens on the floor, or seen there in the last 120 s, go in doubt. Nobody can have come in through a door that did not open, so it only counts people out.
      • Lost at a portal with exactly one destination that is observed or enclosed?
        • yesmove the token there unseen (went through). A stairwell leading both up and down: unknown which, so stay.
        • noA door from here into an unobserved region is open, or changed within INTO_UNOBSERVED_S = 20 s?
          • yesmove the token into that region unseen

10.6 Unobserved regions (garage) and door order

HC-UN-1Into: when a door between an observed region a and an unobserved region b changes, every unseen token in a lost within INTO_UNOBSERVED_S is moved into b. A token lost after the door moved is handled by _lose (§10.5).
HC-UN-2Out of: a new track in a region linked to an unobserved region is a counted arrival when the link's door was used in the last 60 s and one of the unobserved region's exits opened in the last UNOBSERVED_ARRIVE_S = 600 s.
HC-UN-3Direction of an exit window, for an exit of an unobserved region with doors to observed regions only. If any inner door is open, or changed within DIRECTION_S = 90 s before the exit opened, the direction is "out": through the garage-entry door, press the button, the big door opens. Otherwise it is "in": the remote in the car opens the big door first. For observed regions the direction is None (unknown).
  • "out": the floor is put in doubt, but new tracks are not arrivals.
  • "in": nobody is put in doubt; new tracks are arrivals.
  • None: both.

10.7 Unexplained tracks

_recheck(pending track) — every frame of its region
  • Still flagged as a one-radar reflection?
    • yeswait
  • Still within 1500 mm of someone visible?
    • yeswait
  • patient = waited ≥ CONFIRM_S, and it appeared at no portal or at a portal to an enclosed room. (The radar missed them coming out.)
  • _match(region, patient) finds someone?
    • yesbind (someone who went unseen meanwhile)
  • This floor's window unsettled, direction ≠ "out"?
    • yesnew token "arrived", in doubt (seen through the door before it opened)
  • Waited ≥ CONFIRM_S (60 s; BOOTSTRAP_CONFIRM_S = 20 s in the bootstrap hour)?
    • nowait
  • Someone visible in a doorless neighbouring region (possibly the same person seen by two radars), or anyone within 1500 mm in this region?
    • yeswait
    • nonew token: "bootstrap" (counted) in the first hour without saved counts; else "unexplained", provisional
HC-PR-1A provisional token is confirmed (counted) once it is bound in a region other than the first it was seen in, or its total seen time reaches UNEXPLAINED_SEEN_S = 600 s. It is removed "not a person" if neither happens within UNEXPLAINED_EXPIRE_S = 3600 s. Washers, fans and reflections do not walk on.

10.8 Machines: tracks that never move

HC-ST-1For each track, the engine keeps where it appeared and the farthest it has moved from there. A track that is still within STATIC_MM = 400 of where it appeared after STATIC_S = 90 s is static, a machine such as a fan seen through a wall. It is left out of every later frame until it moves ≥ 400 mm, after which it is treated as a new appearance.
A track turns static: give back (_give_back)
  • Was it pending?
    • yesdiscard ("ghost: never moved")
  • Bound to a token? Did that track create the token (origin)?
    • yesremove the token "not a person"
    • norestore the token's before snapshot (region, seen region, position, last seen, doubt). The person goes back to where they were before this track; they are not removed.
HC-ST-2Still spots: the seconds that still tracks spend within SPOT_MM = 500 of a point are added up per region and forgotten SPOT_FORGET_S = 6 h after the spot was last active. SPOT_APPLY = False: spots are only reported (still_spots(), tools/headcount.py --spots), never applied, because applied automatically they also catch a desk.

10.9 Evidence and corrections (engine side)

evidence(region, t, why, move): someone is here, though no track says so
  • Is a counted or in-doubt token in this region visible?
    • yesnothing to add
  • Is someone visible in another region within PORTAL_MM of a portal leading here (reaching in for the switch)?
    • yesthem; nothing
  • Is a token believed to be here?
    • yesthe most recently seen one
    • nomove?
      • yes_match(region): the unseen person who could have walked here (never out of an enclosed room)
      • nonothing (a light switched off confirms, it does not move anyone)
  • Move it here if needed; last_seen = t; log "evidence". If it is in doubt and the floor has no unsettled window: re-establish it. Evidence never adds a person: a switch does not say who.
HC-CO-1correct(region, n): remove tokens beyond n, longest-unseen first and visible ones last (a phantom is someone nobody has seen). Add "corrected" tokens (unseen, seen now) up to n. Re-establish the remaining tokens in doubt.
HC-CO-2correct_home(n): only removes, anywhere, longest-unseen first. Adding is not supported, because who is where is not known. With n = 0 it also removes provisional tokens and clears pending tracks.

In both corrections an anchored token (HC-OC-1) is removed after the other unseen ones and before the visible ones.

10.10 Occupancy: someone held in a region (bed sensors)

hold(region, on, t, why, move): an occupancy sensor's region is held (on) or free (off)
  • on?
    • noRemove the region from _held. If its anchor is still there and unseen: last_seen = t; log "evidence". They just got up, so they are the likeliest next track nearby.
    • yesIs a counted or in-doubt token believed to be here (_anchor_in)?
      • yesanchor = a visible one first, then a counted one before an in-doubt one, then the most recently seen
      • nomove, and nobody visible next door at a portal leading here?
        • yes_match(region), moved here unseen
        • noheld with no anchor (never adds a person)
  • _held[region] = anchor. If unseen: last_seen = t. Log "evidence". If it was in doubt: re-establish it at once, even while the window is open (someone in bed did not go out).
HC-OC-1While a region is held, its anchor is not matched to a track in another region (_match skips it) unless that track appears at a portal leading to the held region (only includes it: coming out through the bedroom door while the sensor lags). The anchor is also never put in doubt (_doubt skips it): not by an exit door opening, a virtual exit, or walking towards the exit.
HC-OC-2On every tick, an anchor that is gone, has left the region or is provisional is replaced by _anchor_in(region) (possibly none). With two people in bed and either side on, one is the anchor; the other is free to be a track elsewhere. Several sensors in one region are one signal: at least one person, never one each.

11. Head count — runtime inputs

Source: headcount_runtime.py

RT-0Every input reaches the engine through _hc(name, *args): frames, doors, pulses, evidence and ticks. This is the single point where vacuum filtering and hold/replay apply (§11.4). A new input path must go through it too.

11.1 Doors to outside and connection doors

  • Door kinds: door covers contacts and covers; "open" means a state in {on, open, opening, closing}. lock covers lock entities configured as outside doors that have no paired door contact. door_lock covers locks paired with a configured door contact on the same device. Pairing wins: a paired lock that is also listed as an outside door is still a door_lock, and never pulses (RT-LK-1).
  • Ignored states: unavailable and unknown are ignored. The event time is the state's last_changed.
  • Start-up: a door that is open at start-up and not locked is fed as opening at start-up.

11.2 Door and lock decision tree

_on_door(event)
  • New state unavailable / unknown?
    • yesignore
  • New state "locked"?
    • yesEach door paired with this lock that the engine believes open: log "off (locked)" and feed the door as closed. A locked door is closed, whatever its contact says.
  • Otherwise, was the old state "locked" (unlocked now)?
    • yesEach paired door whose contact says open while the engine believes it closed: log "<state> (unlocked)" and feed it open. The contact counts again and may already say open.
  • Kind door_lock?
    • yesstop (watched only for the two rules above)
  • Kind door, an open state, and its paired lock is locked?
    • yeslog "ignored: the door is locked, so it is closed"; stop
  • Kind lock?
    • yesIf the old state was known and differs: pulse (open and close at t). This starts and immediately closes an exit window on the lock's floor.
    • nofeed door(entity, state ∈ OPEN_STATES, t)
RT-LK-1A lock on the same device as a configured door contact is that door's lock (door_lock), even when it is also listed as an outside door. The contact says when the door is used; the lock only says it is closed. Before this rule, such a lock pulsed on every lock and unlock. Each pulse put everyone on that floor (or seen there in the last 120 s) in doubt, and anyone then unseen for 10 minutes, such as a sleeper on the entry floor, was removed as "left".
A lock without a paired contact still pulses (EC-74). Use one as an outside door only when the door has no contact sensor.

11.3 Evidence (switches, contacts, buttons)

_on_evidence(event)
  • New state unavailable / unknown?
    • yesignore
  • An event.* entity (button)?
    • yesIs the press time (its state) within EVENT_FRESH_S = 30 s of now?
      • noignore (at start-up it restores its last press)
    • noOld state missing, unavailable or unknown, or unchanged?
      • yesignore
  • Does the context have a parent_id (an automation or script) or a user_id (the app)?
    • yeslog "ignored: by an automation or script / from the app". Only use by hand counts.
  • Listed in evidence_on, and the new state is not "on"?
    • yesignore (a timer may turn it off)
  • A binary_sensor (door contact)?
    • openedwait CONTACT_MIN_S = 2 s. If it is still open then: evidence at the time it opened (move = true).
    • closedWas it open for less than 2 s?
      • yesignore (the magnet flickering at the edge of its range)
  • Log the signal; evidence(region, t, entity, move), with move = false when a light, switch, fan or input_boolean turned off (on the way out: confirms, moves nobody).

11.3b Occupancy sensors (bed sensors)

_on_occupancy(event) → _occupancy_steady(region, t)
  • New state unavailable / unknown?
    • yesignore
  • The region is occupied while any of its occupancy sensors is on (a bed's left, right, both and either sensors are one signal). No context filter: these sensors are not switched by hand.
  • Same as the applied state, with no change waiting?
    • yesnothing
  • Wait OCCUPANCY_STEADY_S = 10 s. A newer change restarts the wait.
  • Still different from the applied state?
    • nonothing (turning over, a bounce)
    • yeslog the signal ("on: someone is there while it stays on" / "off: they got up"); hold(region, on, t of the change, sensor)
RT-OC-1At start-up, every region with a sensor on is held with move = false: it anchors whoever was restored there and moves nobody in. Held state is not saved; it is read back from the sensors.

11.4 Robot vacuums

A robot vacuum moves like a person to a radar: slow sweeps across a whole room. While a configured vacuum is cleaning or returning, the head count ignores the radars of the room it reports. Its room sensor's state is matched to a space whose area name has the same letters and digits (case-insensitive, punctuation and spaces removed).

RT-VAC-1Start of a hold: a vacuum is active but its room is unknown, unavailable or empty. The runtime deep-copies the engine and remembers the log length. Every call through _hc is recorded, and while the hold lasts every track in a frame is flagged unconfirmed: new tracks wait, while continuing and re-found tracks go on.
RT-VAC-2End of a hold: no active vacuum is unsure any more, or the hold has lasted HOLD_MAX_S, or a correction arrives. The runtime restores the copy and drops the log entries written since, except door, signal and ignored entries. It replays the recorded calls with the now-known vacuumed spaces and the real reflection flags, sorts the log tail by time, and logs "replayed N updates".
RT-VAC-3A vacuumed space receives an empty track list every frame. Tracks there are therefore lost at the moment cleaning starts, with the usual §10.5 consequences: portal moves and virtual exits. Zone presence is not affected; the vacuum still turns zones on.

11.5 Reflection flags for overlapping radars

RT-REF-1In a group with ≥2 radars, a fused track seen by exactly one radar is flagged unconfirmed if another available member covers its position with margin: side margin ≥ COVER_MARGIN_MM = 500 and range ≤ max − 500. Another radar looks there and sees nothing, so it is probably a reflection (a kitchen radar seeing someone twice). It explains nobody and creates nobody until another radar also sees it (§10.2, §10.7).

11.6 Persistence, start-up and outputs

  • Saved counts: counts per region (by_region: counted tokens only) are saved 10 s after a change, but only while no window is unsettled. They are saved immediately after a correction, and again at shutdown.
  • Restore: at start-up each saved region gets that many tokens, unseen, with last_seen = now − 1 day, so anyone seen since is preferred when matching. Bootstrap is then off.
  • Bootstrap: without saved counts, the first BOOTSTRAP_S = 1 h is bootstrap. Unexplained tracks become counted people after 20 s, not provisional ones.
  • Decision log: every change, door and signal, each with a snapshot of the tokens. "Seen" snapshots are added at most every 15 s. The log is kept for 3 days, saved with a 120 s delay, and the panel's head count view replays it.
  • Outputs: home_count is None while any floor is unsettled. A floor's count is None while that floor is unsettled. An area's count sums its spaces (an area seen by two groups), and a correction of an area's count goes to the chosen space with the others set to 0.
  • Radars switched off: a radar with headcount = off does not define a region. If it belongs to an aligned group, its observations are still fused into the group's tracks, which the head count receives.

15. Invariants and known limitations

15.1 Invariants

INV-1Counts add up: area counts sum to floor counts, and floor counts to the home count. All are derived from the same counted tokens.
INV-2The home count rises only through:
  • an arrival during an exit window (re-established afterwards);
  • an arrival out of an unobserved region shortly after its exit opened;
  • a provisional person confirmed (walked to another region, or seen for 10 minutes);
  • a bootstrap person;
  • a restore at start-up;
  • a correction.
INV-3The home count falls only through:
  • "left" after an exit window or virtual exit;
  • a token created by a track that turned out static;
  • a correction.
Between SETTLE_S and re-establishment, people in doubt are temporarily not counted.
INV-4Evidence never adds or removes a person. Pending tracks never count. The static filter never removes a person it did not create.
INV-5Zone presence and the head count do not influence each other. Vacuum handling, door rules and evidence affect the head count only.
INV-6The Python engines and their JavaScript ports produce identical transitions on the test vectors.

15.2 Known limitations / not used yet

  • A restart during an exit window drops the people in doubt (EC-91).
  • A lock without a paired contact, configured as an outside door, puts its floor in doubt on every lock and unlock (EC-74).
  • Stair PIR sensors are not used as evidence of floor crossings.
  • An occupancy sensor never adds a person: with nobody unseen who could be there, the region is held with no anchor. If the sensor turns off more than CONFIRM_S after its person was seen leaving (a long off delay), their track meanwhile becomes a provisional person.
  • Still spots are not applied automatically (SPOT_APPLY = False). Ghosts must be excluded via the guide.
  • Guide answers are stored per browser, not on the server.
  • A radar switched off for the head count still contributes tracks to its aligned group.
  • Directions are known only for exits of unobserved regions (the garage). The front door's direction is always unknown.

16.1 Before simplifying

  1. Keep the ✔ scenarios green. They encode the behaviours that were each found by tracing a wrong count in real data.
  2. Write a test first for each ✘ rule you touch. The engine rules all have one now; what is left is mostly geometry and start-up (EC-07, EC-26, EC-53, EC-91), plus the runtime's reflection geometry (RT-REF-1: which radar "looks" at a spot), whose engine side EC-50 covers.
  3. Re-run the private replay and compare hours wrong before and after. The rules were tuned on those same days, so also check new days.
  4. Candidates for simplification, with their risk:
    • ESTABLISH_ON_EXIT_FLOOR and VIRTUAL_EXITS are switches kept for experiments.
    • SPOT_APPLY and the still-spot accumulation are suggestion-only.
    • The engine's still spots duplicate the panel's pinpoint analysis.
    • The door_lock kind versus configuring the lock as an outside door overlap (§11.2).
    • The three-way portal matching in §10.2 is load-bearing: without doorways the same engine was about 38 h wrong on the replayed days.
